What is an AI twin?

A DIGITAL DOUBLE OF YOU

An AI twin is a digital double of a real person that appears in short videos. It is built from that person's own photoshoot, says only the words they have approved, and lets them be present in rooms their calendar will not allow. It is one service from a full studio, so a twin sits beside real shoots, real films and real websites.

How does an AI twin get made?

PHOTOSHOOT TO MESSAGE

1. The conversation

We talk through who the twin is for, where the messages will be used and who signs off on each one. Nothing is built until you say yes.

2. The photoshoot

A dedicated studio session captures you from the angles a twin needs. Real photographs of you are the source, never an AI-generated stand-in.

3. The build and review

Your twin is built and checked against your photographs. You see it before anyone else does.

4. Scripts, then messages

For each message, you approve the script first. Then it is made, edited, captioned and delivered through your private client portal.

What you control

YOUR LIKENESS STAYS YOURS

ALL FAQS →
What is an AI twin?

An AI twin is a digital double of a real person that appears in short videos. It is built from that person's own photoshoot, and it says only the words the person has approved.

Is an AI twin the same as an AI avatar?

Not quite. An AI avatar can be a made-up presenter. An AI twin is a real person's likeness, built from their own photoshoot with their permission, so it is always about one specific person.

Who decides what my twin says?

You do. Every script is approved by you before a message is made, and nothing goes out without your yes.

What do I need to do to get one?

Take part in a dedicated photoshoot, talk through where the messages will be used, and approve each script. Taylormade Creative handles the build and the editing.

How long are the messages?

Short. The working format is 30 to 40 seconds. A longer first cut for Dr. Wallace felt too long, so the format moved to short messages.

How much does an AI twin cost?

An AI twin is quoted per project on scope. Send the details through the booking page and Taylormade Creative replies with a real quote within one business day.
