App questions

Also see web design and all FAQs.

How much does it cost to build an app?

Every app is quoted on its scope: the screens, the features, the platforms and whether it needs accounts, payments or a back end. Send the idea and a budget range and you get a real quote within one business day. A 50% deposit starts the build.

Which platforms do you build for?

Apps for iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac are the focus, and both apps on the App Store include an Apple Watch companion. If you also need Android or a web version, say so in your inquiry and it is scoped in the quote.

Do you handle App Store submission?

Yes. The build is taken through App Store review, including the screenshots, the listing and the privacy details Apple asks for. Run It UP! Dallas and Taylormade Camera both went through that process and are live on the App Store.

How long does an app take?

It depends on the number of screens and features, so the timeline is set in your quote before any deposit. Apple's review usually adds a few days at the end.

Can you also design the website and the launch content?

Yes. The same studio designs websites, shoots commercial video and photography, and makes launch content, so the app, the site and the ads look like one brand.
