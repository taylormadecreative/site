Quick answers

SC.05 — FAQ
How much is a headshot session, and what do I get?

$150 flat, paid online when you book. That's a 30-minute session, one look, the full gallery from the session to choose from, and three retouched finals back within 48 hours of you picking them.

When can I book?

Any day of the week, 9am to 9pm Central. The calendar shows real openings from my live production schedule. Booking closes 24 hours before each session, so the earliest slot you can take is at least a day out.

What should I wear to a headshot session?

Bring the top you want to be seen in, plus one backup. Solid colors photograph best — busy patterns fight your face. Necklines that sit close to the collarbone read cleanest at headshot crop. A prep email with the full checklist arrives before your session.

What does 'one look' mean?

One outfit against one background. Thirty minutes is enough to work that look properly — expression, angle, and light — rather than rushing two looks and nailing neither. Need multiple looks or a longer session? Send an inquiry and you'll get a quote.

Where do we shoot?

At my studio in Downtown Dallas. The exact address comes with your confirmation email once your session is booked.

How do I pick my three retouched images?

The full gallery from your session goes into your own private client portal. You look through it, pick your three favorites, and those come back retouched within 48 hours.

Can I reschedule?

Life happens. Reach out and we'll figure it out together — reply to any email from me or send a message through the client portal, and we'll land on a date that works.

Do you photograph actors and performers too?

Yes. The same session works for actor headshots, dance and performing arts, and personal branding — the direction changes, the price doesn't. If you're submitting to agencies and need casting digitals instead, those are a separate $100 session.
