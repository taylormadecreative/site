Questions, answered

Prices, timelines and how booking works. All FAQs · Every price

How much does a website cost with Taylormade Creative?

Websites come in four flat tiers: an ad landing page is $1,200, a starter business website is $2,000, a premium business website is $3,500, and custom or multi-site builds start at $6,000. A 50% deposit starts the build, and every inquiry gets a reply within one business day.

How much does a commercial or brand video cost?

Every commercial, brand film, product video and social ad is quoted on its scope: the concept, the locations, the crew it needs and how complex the edit is. Send the idea and a budget range and you get a real quote within one business day. A 50% deposit locks the shoot date.

Does Taylormade Creative do commercial photography for brands?

Yes. Campaign, beauty and product photography for hair, beauty, skincare and consumer brands is a core service, art-directed, lit and retouched in-house at a downtown Dallas studio or on location. Clients include TONI&GUY, Oribe, Neutrogena, Black Girl Sunscreen, Goldwell and MYX.

Can Taylormade Creative design and build an app?

Yes. Taylormade Creative designs and builds apps for iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac, then takes them through App Store review. Run It UP! Dallas and Taylormade Camera are both live on the App Store. App projects are quoted on scope within one business day.

How long does a project take?

Photo and video projects deliver in about two weeks after the shoot, with two rounds of edits included, through a private client portal. Website and app timelines depend on scope, so they are set in your quote before any deposit.

Where is Taylormade Creative, and do you travel?

The studio is in downtown Dallas and shoots on location across Dallas–Fort Worth, including Plano, Frisco, Irving, Allen, McKinney, Southlake, Westlake, Arlington and Fort Worth. Projects outside DFW are welcome; travel is added to the quote.

Who is behind Taylormade Creative?

Nelson Taylor, who also goes by Elliott Taylor, founded Taylormade Creative and directs every project. He brings 14 years of creative direction and graphic design, and the work is designed, shot, edited and built in-house.

Do you sign NDAs and include usage rights?

Yes. NDAs are signed on request, and corporate videos come captioned in horizontal and vertical versions with usage rights included. Raw footage is available for an extra fee. Headshots, digitals, and senior and birthday photoshoots can also be booked and paid for online in a minute.
