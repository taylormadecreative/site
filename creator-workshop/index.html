SC 01The problem

Your content already deserves better

  • It looks fine in the room and flat on the screen
  • After the sun goes down, everything turns to mush
  • People scroll off before you finish the first sentence
  • The audio gets you skipped before the shot ever does
  • One thirty second clip eats your whole evening

None of that is a camera problem. It's a setup problem, and a setup can be taught in one night.

Sound that keeps them watching

SC 03The room

A bare loft until somebody lights it

Two rooms, hardwood, tall windows, downtown out the glass. Nothing about it is special until the lights go up. That is the whole lesson.

Same floor. Same windows. The only thing that changed is what is pointed at it, and that is a thing you can learn in one night and use forever.

SC 04Run of show

Two hours, two rooms, everybody shoots

  1. 7:00 PM

    You leave with footage on your phone and the numbers of the people you just shot with.

SC 08Before you book

The questions people ask

Do I need a camera or any gear?

No. Bring your phone charged, and headphones if you use them. Everything is demonstrated on setups that are already running in the room.

I am a beginner. Is this over my head?

No. If you have posted a video before, you are ready. Hour two is hands on with somebody standing next to you.

Where exactly is it?

A private studio in Dallas. The exact address goes out in your ticket confirmation email. It is not listed publicly.

What should I wear?

Whatever you would actually wear on camera. You are going to be filmed, and you are keeping the footage.

Will I leave with something usable?

Yes. Hour two is you shooting on the setups. Whatever you capture is yours to post.

Can I transfer or refund my ticket?

Life happens. Email hello@taylormadecreative.net and we will sort it out. Tickets are managed through Eventbrite.

Is there a group rate?

Reach out before you book if you are bringing a team: hello@taylormadecreative.net.
