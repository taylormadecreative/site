Quick answers

SC.04 — FAQ
How much are senior photos?

Two options, both paid in full when you book. The mini session is $150: 30 minutes, one look, and 3 edited photos. The full session is $350: one hour, two or more looks, and 8 edited photos. Same price in the studio or on location anywhere in DFW.

Can we shoot on location?

Yes. Pick On location when you book and tell me where: a park, a street downtown, your neighborhood, a spot that means something to you. Anywhere in Dallas–Fort Worth, same price as the studio.

Why do on-location sessions show fewer times?

I keep an hour clear on each side of an on-location session for the drive, so those times fit into fewer gaps on my calendar than studio times do.

What counts as a look?

A look is one outfit. The mini session is one outfit worked properly for 30 minutes. The full session gives you an hour, so you can change into two or more outfits, like your cap and gown plus something that's all you.

How do I choose my edited photos?

After the shoot, a proofing gallery goes live in your private client portal. You pick your favorites (3 on the mini session, 8 on the full session) and those are the ones I edit.

What should I bring?

Your outfits, plus anything that tells your story: your cap and gown, a letterman jacket, a jersey, an instrument, the shirt for the college you're headed to. Tell me what you're bringing when you book so I can plan around it.

Can a parent book for their senior?

Yes. Book with your own name and email and add your senior's name on the details step. The confirmation, the prep email, and the proofing gallery all go to the email you book with.

Where is the studio?

Downtown Dallas. The exact address comes with your confirmation email once your studio session is booked.

When can I book?

The calendar shows real openings from my live production schedule, 9am to 9pm every day. Online booking closes 24 hours before each session.

Can I reschedule?

Life happens. Reach out and we'll figure it out together: reply to any email from me or send a message through the client portal, and we'll land on a date that works.
