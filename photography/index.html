All FAQs
Do you shoot commercial campaigns for brands?

Yes. Taylormade Creative shoots campaign images for hair, beauty, skincare and consumer brands, including TONI&GUY, Oribe, Neutrogena, Black Girl Sunscreen, Goldwell, MYX, R+Co and KMS. Art direction, lighting and retouching are done in-house, and images are delivered for ads, e-commerce and social. Campaigns are quoted per project within one business day, and a 50% deposit locks the date.

How much does commercial photography cost in Dallas?

Commercial campaigns, beauty, product and branding shoots are quoted per project because scope drives the cost: send an inquiry and you get a real number within one business day. A 50% deposit locks your date. Sessions book instantly online: headshots are $150 flat, digitals are $100 flat, and senior photos and birthday photoshoots are $150 (mini) or $350 (full). Team headshots for companies are $125 per person at your office or $99 per person in the studio, with a 10-person minimum.

Do you photograph med spas and beauty brands?

Yes. Beauty work covers skin, hair and makeup close-ups and product in hand for beauty brands, plus provider portraits, team photos and promos for med spas. It is quoted per project within one business day.

How fast do I get my photos?

For quoted projects, edited finals are delivered in your private client portal in about two weeks, with two rounds of edits included. Headshot finals come back within 48 hours of picking them.

How many photos do I get?

After the shoot, a proofing gallery goes live in your private client portal. You select your favorites and those selects get the full edit. For campaigns, product and branding shoots, the final count is set in your quote, because scope drives it. Headshots are a fixed three retouched finals, and senior photos and birthday photoshoots are 3 edited photos (mini) or 8 (full).

Do you shoot in studio or on location?

Both. Shoots happen in the downtown Dallas studio or on location: your space, a venue, or the streets of DFW. Travel gets scoped into your quote. Senior photos are the same price in the studio or on location anywhere in DFW.
