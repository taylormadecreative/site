How much do headshots and photo sessions cost in Dallas–Fort Worth?

Headshots are $150 flat, digitals are $100 flat, and senior photos and birthday photoshoots are $150 (mini) or $350 (full). All of them book instantly online, no quote needed. Team headshots for companies are $125 per person at your office or $99 per person in the studio, with a 10-person minimum. Branding photoshoots and product photography are quoted per project because scope drives the cost: send an inquiry and you get a real number within one business day. A 50% deposit locks your date.

What should I wear to my photoshoot?

Your prep email covers it a few days before the shoot. The short version: solid colors photograph best, and bringing 2–3 options gives the session range.

How many photos do I get?

After the shoot, a proofing gallery goes live in your private client portal. You select your favorites and those selects get the full edit. Headshots are a fixed three retouched finals, and senior photos and birthday photoshoots are 3 edited photos (mini) or 8 (full). For branding photoshoots and product photography, the final count is set in your quote, because scope drives it.

How fast do I get my photos?

Headshot finals come back within 48 hours of picking them. For quoted projects, edited finals are delivered in your private client portal in about two weeks, with two rounds of edits included.

Do you shoot in studio or on location?

Both. Shoots happen in the downtown Dallas studio or on location: your space, a venue, or the streets of DFW. Travel gets scoped into your quote. Senior photos are the same price in the studio or on location anywhere in DFW.
