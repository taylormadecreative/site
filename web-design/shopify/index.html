Stay on Shopify, or go custom?

An honest answer either way. Both are designed and built here, and the quote says which fits.

Shopify fits when

  • You sell physical products and want Shopify's checkout, payments and shipping
  • Your team adds products and runs orders every day
  • You want apps for reviews, subscriptions or local delivery

Custom code fits when

  • Your site is mostly booking, services or content, not a catalog
  • You want no monthly platform fee
  • The site has to do something no theme or app does

Also see custom web design, WordPress, Squarespace, Wix design, and all FAQs.

Do you build Shopify themes from scratch?

Yes. A custom theme can be designed and built for your store, or your current theme can be redesigned section by section. Either way it is built as an unpublished theme beside your live one and switched on when you approve it.

How much does Shopify web design cost?

Shopify work is quoted on scope: the number of page templates, sections and features. Send your store link and what you want changed, and you get a real quote within one business day. A 50% deposit starts the build.

Will my store stay open during the redesign?

Yes. The new theme is built unpublished while your current theme keeps selling, and it only goes live when you approve it.

Can you shoot product photos for my store?

Yes. Taylormade Creative is also a commercial photo and video studio in Dallas, so product photos and launch video can be quoted with the store design and shot to fit the layout.

Should I move from Shopify to a custom site?

Usually not if you sell products: Shopify's checkout, payments and shipping are hard to beat. A custom build makes more sense for service businesses, booking-based businesses and brands whose site is mostly content.
