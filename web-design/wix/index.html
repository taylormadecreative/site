Stay on Wix, or go custom?

An honest answer either way. Both are designed and built here, and the quote says which fits.

Wix fits when

  • You want to edit text and photos yourself in a visual editor
  • You use Wix tools like Events, Bookings or Stores
  • A monthly plan that covers hosting is fine with you

Custom code fits when

  • You want faster pages and no monthly platform fee
  • The site needs accounts, dashboards or a custom player
  • You want a design built from zero

Wix questions

Also see custom web design, Shopify, WordPress, Squarespace design, and all FAQs.

Can you redesign my Wix site?

Yes. The redesign is done in your own Wix account, page by page, including the separate phone view, and you keep editing it yourself afterward.

How much does Wix web design cost?

Wix work is quoted on scope: how many pages, sections and features. Send your site and what you want changed, and you get a real quote within one business day.

Can you set up Wix Events and forms?

Yes. Events, RSVPs and forms are set up inside Wix as part of the design, so sign-ups land in your Wix dashboard.

Should I move from Wix to a custom site?

Stay if the Wix editor and its tools do what you need. A custom build makes sense when you want faster pages, no monthly platform fee, or features Wix can't do well.
