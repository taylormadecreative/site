Website questions

Also see Shopify, WordPress, Squarespace and Wix design, app design and all FAQs.

How much does a website cost in Dallas–Fort Worth?

Custom websites are priced in four flat tiers: an Ad Landing Page is $1,200, a Starter Business Website is $2,000, a Premium Business Website is $3,500, and custom or multi-site builds start at $6,000. Every build starts with a 50% deposit, and every inquiry gets a real quote within one business day.

Do you design on Shopify, WordPress, Squarespace or Wix?

Yes. Taylormade Creative designs custom Shopify themes, redesigns WordPress sites, and designs Squarespace and Wix sites past their templates. Platform work is quoted on scope within one business day. If you would rather own the code outright, a hand-coded custom build is the other option.

What is the difference between a custom-coded site and Squarespace or Wix?

A custom-coded site is designed from zero and written by hand, so it loads fast, can do whatever the business needs, like booking, accounts or a music player, and has no monthly platform fee. Squarespace and Wix are easier to edit yourself and come with a monthly plan. Both can look custom; the right choice depends on who will update the site and what it has to do.

How long does a website take to build?

It depends on the tier and how much content is being built, so the timeline is set in your quote before any deposit. You know what you are getting and when before the build starts.

Do I own my website?

Yes. With a custom build, the design, the code and the content are yours when it is done, with no platform subscription needed to keep it online. On Shopify, WordPress, Squarespace or Wix, the site lives in your own account.

Will my website work on phones?

Every page is laid out for phones, tablets and desktops and checked on each before launch. Most people will meet your business on a phone first, so that is where the design starts.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Send the current site and what is not working. A move to a custom build is scoped against the four tiers, a redesign that stays on your platform is quoted on scope, and either way you get a real quote within one business day.

Can my website take bookings or payments?

Yes. Custom builds can include online booking with real availability, client accounts and checkout. Jenel Studios takes bookings and payments on its own site, and Sitting Pretty Rashae's runs client bookings, accounts and an owner dashboard.

Do you handle SEO and copywriting?

On-page SEO is built into every tier: clean structure, fast pages, structured data and FAQs written for how people and AI assistants search. Copywriting can be scoped into your quote if you need the words written for you.

Do you build websites outside Dallas?

Yes. Taylormade Creative is based in Dallas and builds for businesses across Dallas–Fort Worth, including Fort Worth, Plano, Frisco, Irving, Allen, McKinney and Southlake. Web projects run online, so the business can be anywhere.
