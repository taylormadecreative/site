Stay on WordPress, or go custom?

An honest answer either way. Both are designed and built here, and the quote says which fits.

WordPress fits when

  • You publish a blog, news or a lot of pages
  • Several people edit the site
  • You rely on plugins for forms, events or memberships

Custom code fits when

  • The site is a handful of pages that rarely change
  • You want faster pages and nothing to update or patch
  • You would rather have no plugins to keep up with

WordPress questions

Also see custom web design, Shopify, Squarespace, Wix design, and all FAQs.

Can you redesign my WordPress site without losing my content?

Yes. The redesign is built on your existing WordPress install, so your posts, pages and media stay where they are, and the old pages are kept as a backup. Helping Us Help Her's new pages were built this way, inside its existing WordPress site. You approve the new design before it goes live.

How much does a WordPress redesign cost?

WordPress work is quoted on scope: how many page layouts change, and whether speed and SEO cleanup is included. Send your site and what is not working, and you get a real quote within one business day.

Why is my WordPress site slow?

The usual causes are oversized images, too many plugins and heavy page builders loading code on every page. A cleanup starts with the biggest weight and works down.

Should I move off WordPress?

If you publish often or rely on plugins, staying on WordPress usually makes sense. If the site is a few pages that rarely change, a hand-coded custom site is faster and has nothing to update.
