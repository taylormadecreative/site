Stay on Squarespace, or go custom?

An honest answer either way. Both are designed and built here, and the quote says which fits.

Squarespace fits when

  • You want to make your own edits without a developer
  • You need a portfolio, a blog or a simple shop
  • A monthly plan that covers hosting is fine with you

Custom code fits when

  • The site needs booking, accounts or features the editor doesn't have
  • You want faster pages and no monthly platform fee
  • You want a design no template can get to

Squarespace questions

Also see custom web design, Shopify, WordPress, Wix design, and all FAQs.

Can you redesign my Squarespace site?

Yes. Your site can be redesigned inside your own Squarespace account with custom fonts, sections and code, and you keep editing it yourself afterward.

How much does Squarespace web design cost?

Squarespace work is quoted on scope: how many pages and how much custom design and code. Send your site and what you want changed, and you get a real quote within one business day.

Can a Squarespace site look custom?

Yes. With your own type and color, custom code and layouts built past the template, a Squarespace site can look like a custom build. JaQuandice's official site is one example.

Should I move from Squarespace to a custom site?

Stay if you like editing it yourself and the editor does what you need. Move if the site needs booking, accounts or speed the platform can't give you, or you would rather stop paying a monthly plan.
