How does an AI commercial get made?

SCRIPT TO SCREEN

1. The script

You send the idea and any reference images. I write the script: the scenes, what happens in each, and every line that gets said.

2. The storyboard

Every scene is drawn out frame by frame, with the cast and the locations locked. You approve the storyboard before production starts.

3. The shots

Each shot is generated and directed one at a time, with the same cast and locations carried through every scene, so it holds together as one film.

4. The finish

The cut, the color grade, sound and music. Your film is delivered through your private client portal at the length and resolution you ordered.

ALL FAQS →
What is AI video production?

AI video production is making a commercial or an ad with generative AI in place of a camera crew: the people, the places and every shot are generated, then directed, edited and scored like any other film. At Taylormade Creative a creative director writes the script, storyboards it for your approval and directs every shot.

How much does an AI commercial cost?

Every AI video is quoted on its scope. The two things that move the price most are the length of the video and the resolution it is delivered in. Send the idea through the booking page and Taylormade Creative replies within one business day with a real quote.

What do you need from me to start an AI video?

The idea, where the video will run, and any reference images, logos or product photos you already have. From there Taylormade Creative writes the script and builds a storyboard for you to approve before production starts.

Will it look like my brand, or like generic AI?

Like your brand. Every AI video is art-directed to your brand guide: your colors, type, voice and visual standards, by a creative director with 14 years of brand work. If a shot would not pass a human art review, it does not ship.

Can you make an AI spokesperson for my business?

Yes. An AI spokesperson is a consistent on-camera presenter built for your brand who can deliver a new script whenever you need one, without booking talent every time.

Can AI video be mixed with real footage?

Yes. Taylormade Creative shoots real footage on the Sony FX6 and stills on the Sony A7RV, then uses AI where it serves the story: extending sets, building worlds and turning one shoot day into a full campaign.

Do I own what's made?

Yes. Deliverables are yours: the videos, the imagery and the content the system produces belong to your business, delivered through a private client portal.

Taylormade Creative, a Dallas creative studio, makes AI commercials, AI ads for social, concept films and AI spokespeople for businesses in Dallas–Fort Worth and anywhere else. A creative director with 14 years of brand work writes, storyboards and directs each one in-house.
