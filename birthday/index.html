Quick answers

SC.04 — FAQ
How much is a birthday photoshoot?

Two options, both booked online with a 50% deposit. The mini shoot is $150: 30 minutes, one look, and 3 edited photos. The full shoot is $350: one hour, two or more looks, and 8 edited photos.

How does the deposit work?

You pay 50% when you book: $75 for the mini shoot or $175 for the full shoot. That holds your date. The other 50% is charged automatically to the same card the day before your shoot (right away if you book less than a day ahead), and you get an email receipt. If that charge doesn't go through, I'll email you a link to pay it in your client portal.

What counts as a look?

A look is one outfit. The mini shoot is one outfit worked properly for 30 minutes. The full shoot gives you an hour, so you can change into two or more outfits.

How do I choose my edited photos?

After the shoot, a proofing gallery goes live in your private client portal. You pick your favorites (3 on the mini shoot, 8 on the full shoot) and those are the ones I edit.

Can I bring props like balloons, a crown, or a cake?

Yes. Bring whatever makes it feel like your birthday: balloons, a crown or sash, a cake, flowers, a sign with your number. Tell me what you're bringing when you book so I can plan the set around it.

Where do we shoot?

At my studio in Downtown Dallas. The exact address comes with your confirmation email once your session is booked.

When can I book?

The calendar shows real openings from my live production schedule. Online booking closes 24 hours before each session, so book a few days before your birthday to get the time you want.

Can I reschedule?

Life happens. Reach out and we'll figure it out together: reply to any email from me or send a message through the client portal, and we'll land on a date that works.
