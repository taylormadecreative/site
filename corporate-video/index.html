I come to your office anywhere in DFW, or your team comes to the downtown Dallas studio. I help write or tighten the script and run a teleprompter on set, so nobody has to memorize lines.

ALL FAQS →
How much does a corporate video cost in Dallas?

Every corporate video is quoted per project, because the number of people on camera, the number of videos, the location, and the edit set the cost. Send your brief and you get a real quote within one business day. A 50% deposit locks your date.

Can you film at our office?

Yes. I film at your office anywhere in Dallas–Fort Worth, or your team can come to the downtown Dallas studio.

Do you travel outside Dallas–Fort Worth?

Yes. I travel for shoots outside DFW, and travel is added to your quote.

Do you film customer testimonial videos?

Yes. Your customers tell their story on camera at your office or the downtown Dallas studio, for your website, sales decks, and ads. Quoted per project within one business day.

Do you help with scripts?

Yes. I help write or tighten your script and run a teleprompter on set, so nobody on camera has to memorize lines.

What do we get?

Edited videos with captions, in horizontal and vertical versions, with usage rights included. Raw footage is available for an extra fee.

How many videos can you shoot in one day?

It depends on the job: how many people are on camera, how many videos you need, and how long each one runs. Your quote spells out what fits in the day.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. I'll sign your NDA before the shoot.

What is a content day?

One shoot day at your location. I direct and shoot on the Sony FX6 and Sony A7RV, then edit a set of vertical videos for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts plus a bank of stills from the same day, built to run for a quarter. Two rounds of edits, about two weeks to delivery.

Do you record video podcasts?

Yes, for companies and creators. Hosts and guests record on camera in the downtown Dallas studio, and I edit the full episode plus short clips for social. Episode count is set in your quote.

How long until we get our videos?

About two weeks after the shoot, with two rounds of edits included, delivered through a private client portal. Rush timelines can be scoped in your quote.

Can we get team headshots on the same booking?

Ask for both in your brief. Team headshots are $125 per person at your office or $99 per person at the studio, with a 10-person minimum.
