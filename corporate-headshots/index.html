How much do corporate headshots cost in Dallas?

$125 per person on-site at your office anywhere in DFW, or $99 per person at the downtown Dallas studio, with a 10-person minimum. Ten people is $1,250 on-site or $990 in the studio. Each person gets one retouched headshot, and extra retouched photos are $35 each.

Do you do headshot booths at events?

Yes. I set up a headshot booth at conferences and company events in DFW, and attendees step in for a quick headshot. Booths are quoted per event: send the event, date, venue, expected headcount, and hours.

Do you come to our office?

Yes. I set up at your office anywhere in Dallas–Fort Worth, including Plano, Frisco, Irving, Allen, McKinney, Southlake and Westlake, for $125 per person. If it's easier to come downtown, the studio rate is $99 per person.

Do you travel outside Dallas–Fort Worth?

Yes. The $125 per person on-site rate covers offices anywhere in DFW, and I travel beyond it too. Travel outside DFW is added to your quote.

What if someone misses shoot day?

They can still get their headshot. Anyone who misses the team shoot can be photographed later, and we'll schedule it together.

What if fewer people show up than we booked?

You pay for the headcount you book. If you book 10 people and 8 show up, the bill is for 10.

What's the minimum for team headshots?

10 people. For fewer than 10, individual headshots are $150 flat with three retouched finals, booked online.

What does each person get?

One retouched headshot per person. The gallery from the shoot goes into a private client portal, you choose one frame per person, and those come back retouched within 5 business days of your picks.

What editing add-ons do you offer?

Extra retouched photos are $35 each. Advanced retouching (glasses glare, flyaway hair, deeper skin cleanup) is $25 per photo. A background change to a brand color or a different tone is $15 per photo. Rush delivery, with retouched photos back within 48 hours of your picks, adds 25% to the order.

Will everyone's headshots match?

Yes. The whole team is shot in one setup, so your team page, LinkedIn profiles, and badges look like one set.

How do we book team headshots?

Send your team size, dates, and location through the booking page. You get a quote within one business day. A 50% deposit locks your date, and the balance is due on shoot day.
