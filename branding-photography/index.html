Branding shoots, answered

How much does a branding photoshoot cost in Dallas?

Branding photoshoots are quoted per project, because the number of looks, locations and final images drives the cost. Send what you need and you get a real quote within one business day. A 50% deposit locks your date.

What is a branding photoshoot?

A shoot built around your business instead of one portrait: you, your work, your product and your space, planned as one set so your website, socials and press kit all pull from the same library.

How many photos do I get?

The final count is set in your quote, because scope drives it. Proofs go to your private client portal, you choose the selects, and those get the full edit.

Where do branding shoots happen?

In the downtown Dallas studio, at your business, or on location anywhere in DFW. Travel gets scoped into your quote.

How fast do I get my photos?

Edited finals land in your private client portal in about two weeks, with two rounds of edits included.
