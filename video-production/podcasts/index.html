Video podcast production in Dallas Edited
like a show.

Taylormade Creative produces video podcasts for companies and creators in downtown Dallas. I record each episode on camera at the studio and edit it like a show: a cold open, punch-ins, word-by-word captions, name cards and chapters, plus vertical reels with covers and a YouTube kit. Quotes go out within one business day.

Anatomy of an episode.

A produced episode is built from these parts, and the clip at the top shows them in Auntie PB, Episode 1.

PartWhat it does
Cold openThe best moments first, so viewers know why to stay.
Name cardsWho's speaking, on screen in the first seconds.
Punch-insA tighter crop on whoever's talking, so the picture keeps moving.
Word-by-word captionsTimed to each word, so the episode plays with the sound off.
Chapter titlesThe topic, labeled on screen as the talk moves on.
Brand graphicsYour logo and colors on the cards and titles.
End cardA branded close for the episode.

What you get.

Each episode can come back as the full edit, a pack of vertical reels and a YouTube kit, with the counts set in your quote.

  • The full episode. Edited with every part above and color graded by hand.
  • Vertical reels. Short 9:16 cuts of the best moments for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, each with a cover and captions.
  • A YouTube kit. The thumbnail, titles, description and chapters, written for the upload.

How many reels each episode gets is set in your quote. For reference, Auntie PB, Episode 1 has five.

Companies and creators.

Video podcast production at Taylormade Creative is for companies that want a show of their own and creators building theirs.

Questions, answered

Updated October 2026. Also see all video production, video on retainer and all FAQs.

How much does video podcast production cost in Dallas?

Taylormade Creative quotes every video podcast on scope, so there is no flat price. The quote is built from the show's format, how often you record, the reels you need and how complex the edit is. Send a short brief and Nelson Taylor replies with a real quote within one business day. A 50% deposit locks your recording date.

Where is a video podcast recorded?

Taylormade Creative records video podcasts on camera at its studio in downtown Dallas, with the set lit in-house by Nelson Taylor. Auntie PB, Episode 1, the episode on this page, was recorded there on three cameras. Quotes go out within one business day.

What's the difference between podcast production and a Jenel Studios session?

With Taylormade Creative podcast production, Nelson Taylor records your show on camera and edits each episode with a cold open, punch-ins, captions and chapters, plus vertical reels and a YouTube kit. For podcast sessions by the hour at Jenel Studios, which Nelson also runs, book at jenelstudios.com. To record on your own Mac instead, Taylormade Podcast is a free app in beta.

What comes with each video podcast episode?

A video podcast edit from Taylormade Creative can come with the full episode, a pack of vertical reels with covers and captions, and a YouTube kit with the thumbnail, titles, description and chapters. The full edit includes a cold open, punch-ins, word-by-word captions, name cards in the first seconds, chapter titles and your brand graphics.

How many reels do I get per episode?

The number of vertical reels per episode is set in your Taylormade Creative quote. Tell Nelson Taylor how often you post between episodes and the count is built around that. Each reel is a 9:16 cut with its own cover and captions. For reference, Auntie PB, Episode 1 has five vertical reels.

How long until my episode is ready?

Standard delivery at Taylormade Creative is about two weeks after the shoot, with two rounds of edits included. You get a quote within one business day of sending your brief, and a 50% deposit locks the recording date. Recording every month? Ask about a retainer.
