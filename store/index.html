Good to know

Questions about a shoot or a project? See all FAQs.

Are the apps free?

Yes. Taylormade Camera is free on the App Store, and Taylormade Podcast for Mac is free while it's in beta.

What do I need to run them?

Taylormade Camera needs an iPhone on iOS 17 or later, and an Apple Watch can start and stop the recording. Taylormade Podcast needs macOS 15 Sequoia or later, on an Apple silicon or Intel Mac. Transcripts, and the captions on clips and reels, need macOS 26. The AI drafts (titles, show notes, clip captions and suggested clips) also need Apple Intelligence turned on, on a Mac that supports it.

Is the Mac download safe?

Yes. Taylormade Podcast is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, which checks it for malware before it opens on your Mac.

Where do my recordings go?

They stay on your device. Taylormade Camera keeps what you shoot and edit on your iPhone unless you choose to share it, and Taylormade Podcast keeps your recordings on your Mac. Neither app has accounts, tracking, analytics or ads. Read the Camera and Podcast privacy policies.

Can you build an app for my business?

Yes. The studio that made these apps designs and builds apps for iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac for clients, quoted on scope within one business day. See app design.
