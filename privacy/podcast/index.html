Taylormade Podcast Mac app Last updated October 6, 2026

Your episodes stay on your Mac.

Taylormade Podcast is a Mac app for recording video podcasts, made by Taylormade Creative. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does and doesn't do with your data. The short version: there's no account and no tracking, and everything you record stays on your Mac.

The short version

NO ACCOUNT · NO TRACKING · NO ADS
Does Taylormade Podcast collect any of my data?

No. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service in the app. Your videos, audio, transcripts and settings are saved only on your Mac. Taylormade Creative never receives them.

Does the app use the internet at all?

Only in the few ways listed in the next section, and none of them send your recordings anywhere.

When it goes online

EVERY CONNECTION THE APP MAKES
Checking for a new beta

When it opens (and when you choose Check for Updates), the app asks this website for the newest beta's version number. The request includes the app's own version. Like any web page, the site's hosting sees your internet address. Nothing else is sent.

iPhones on your Wi-Fi (Taylormade Camera)

If you use iPhones running Taylormade Camera as cameras, the Mac finds them on your own Wi-Fi network. Each iPhone pairs once by scanning a code, and the connection between them is encrypted. Video goes straight from the iPhone to your Mac, not through the internet.

ChatGPT "Write it for me" (only if you turn it on)

The teleprompter can ask ChatGPT to draft a script. This only works if you paste in your own OpenAI key. When you use it, the topic you typed is sent to OpenAI under your key, and OpenAI's own privacy policy applies. Nothing else from the app is sent. The key is saved in your Mac's keychain.

Sending beta feedback

Help › Send Beta Feedback opens a new email in your own email app. It's filled in with the app version, your macOS version, your Mac model and what macOS reports about your connected cameras. You can read and change everything before you send it, and nothing is sent unless you press Send.

What the app asks permission for

PERMISSIONS · WHY EACH ONE EXISTS
Camera and microphone

To show and record your podcast cameras and mics. Recordings are saved to the folder you choose on your Mac.

Speech recognition

To write a transcript of your episode, which powers clip captions and suggested clips. It runs on your Mac with Apple's on-device speech recognition. Episode titles and show notes use Apple Intelligence on your Mac, when it's available.

Local network

To find iPhones running Taylormade Camera on your Wi-Fi, as described above. Without it, everything else still works.

Where your episodes live

ON YOUR MAC · YOUR CONTROL
Where are my recordings stored?

In your Movies folder (Movies › Taylormade Podcast) unless you pick another folder. If you turn on a backup copy, the app also copies each episode to the drive you choose.

How do I delete them?

Delete an episode in the app (it moves to the Trash) or delete its folder in Finder. There's no other copy to ask about, because the app never sends one anywhere.

Guest agreements

If you ask guests to sign the agreement in the app, the signed PDF is saved with that episode on your Mac.

A few more things

CHILDREN · CHANGES · CONTACT
Children's privacy

Taylormade Podcast isn't directed at children and doesn't collect information from anyone.

Will this policy change?

If it does, the date at the top of this page changes and the new version is posted here, at the same address.
