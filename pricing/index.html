How much is a branding or product photoshoot?

Both are quoted, since the number of looks, locations, and products sets the cost. If you need something with a flat price today, headshots ($150), digitals ($100), and birthday shoots ($150 or $350) book instantly.

How much do social media content and AI content cost?

Both are quoted. Social media content runs as monthly retainers for reels, carousels, and strategy. AI content covers AI avatars, generated campaigns, and automated content systems. Send an inquiry and you get a quote within one business day.

How do deposits work on quoted projects?

A 50% deposit locks your date, and the balance is due on shoot day. Online sessions work differently: headshots, digitals, senior photos, and the full birthday shoot are paid in full when you book, and the mini birthday shoot takes a $75 deposit with the other $75 charged to the same card the day before.

How long until I get my photos or video?

Quoted projects take about two weeks, with two rounds of edits included, delivered through your private client portal. Headshot finals come back within 48 hours of picking them.
